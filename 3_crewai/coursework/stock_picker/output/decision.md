Chosen company: **Broadcom**

## Why Broadcom was selected
Broadcom stands out as the best investment among the three companies because it offers the strongest combination of **AI infrastructure exposure, visibility into future revenue, and business durability** as of **2026-09-30**.

### 1) Strongest near- and medium-term AI revenue outlook
The most important differentiator is that Reuters reported on **Sept. 2, 2026** that Broadcom raised its **fiscal 2027 AI chip revenue forecast to about $115 billion from $100 billion**. That is a major signal that demand from hyperscale customers is not just healthy but accelerating. Among the three names, Broadcom has the clearest evidence of a substantial, expanding revenue pipeline tied directly to AI infrastructure spending.

### 2) Best balance of growth and visibility
Broadcom is not simply a “story stock” tied to AI enthusiasm. It has:
- deep relationships with hyperscalers,
- a strong position in **custom AI chips**,
- meaningful exposure to **networking** and full-stack infrastructure,
- and a reputation for execution.

That combination gives it better earnings visibility than a more speculative AI entrant and more structural diversification than a narrower pure-play chip bet.

### 3) High-quality AI beneficiary with strategic moat
Broadcom’s moat is reinforced by its role in:
- custom accelerators,
- Ethernet networking,
- and broader infrastructure software/hardware integration.

This makes it one of the most important non-Nvidia beneficiaries of AI buildout. It is positioned where capital spending is flowing now: not just training chips, but the broader systems that connect and scale AI workloads.

### 4) Attractive compounding profile
Even though valuation may already reflect much of the AI optimism, Broadcom still appears best suited for **long-term compounding** because of:
- high margins,
- recurring demand characteristics,
- strategic customer relationships,
- and broad participation in AI infrastructure spend.

As of **2026-09-30**, its outlook is the strongest of the three on a risk-adjusted basis.

## Why the others were not selected

### Advanced Micro Devices
AMD is a strong company and a major AI beneficiary, but it was not the best pick here.

#### Strengths
- It has clearly strengthened its market position.
- Reuters reported it reached a **$1 trillion valuation on Sept. 21, 2026**, reflecting strong investor confidence.
- It has meaningful exposure to AI computing across data center, client, and embedded markets.

#### Why it was not chosen
- AMD’s opportunity is still highly dependent on **execution against faster rivals**, especially Nvidia and Broadcom.
- The company’s upside is more sensitive to **valuation** and market sentiment.
- While its AI positioning is impressive, the research suggests more uncertainty in converting enthusiasm into durable revenue and margin expansion than Broadcom’s clearer demand visibility.

#### Bottom line
AMD is compelling for growth investors, but Broadcom offers a more durable, better-supported AI infrastructure thesis with stronger revenue visibility.

---

### Qualcomm
Qualcomm became more interesting because of its reported partnership with Amazon, but it remains behind Broadcom in overall investment quality.

#### Strengths
- Reuters reported on **Sept. 8, 2026** that Amazon could buy up to **$60 billion** of its AI data-center chips and related products.
- That materially improves Qualcomm’s credibility in AI infrastructure.
- It also offers a diversified story beyond smartphones, which is strategically important.

#### Why it was not chosen
- Qualcomm’s AI opportunity is still **less proven** than Broadcom’s.
- The Amazon deal is promising, but it remains to be seen how much of that headline translates into **recurring, broad-based revenue**.
- The company still has a strong dependence on its core mobile business, so the AI contribution is not yet as central or visible as Broadcom’s.
- Customer concentration and execution risk appear higher relative to the certainty Broadcom has with multiple hyperscaler relationships.

#### Bottom line
Qualcomm is a credible emerging AI beneficiary, but its investment case is still more dependent on future execution and partnership conversion than Broadcom’s established infrastructure position.
